About 12 Play Testers

12 Play Testers is a KubeAI service. It runs Google Play's required closed test for Android apps: 12 human testers on real devices, using your app every day for 14 consecutive days, managed end to end.

Why the service exists

Google asks new personal developer accounts to run a closed test with at least 12 opted-in testers for 14 consecutive days before they can apply for production access. For solo developers building apps with AI, that stretch is the least glamorous part of shipping: recruiting real testers, keeping them engaged every day, and replacing the ones who go quiet. We run that stretch so developers can keep building.

How we work

  • Real people on real Android phones. No bots, emulators or inactive accounts.
  • Anyone who drops off is replaced at no extra cost, and the run is followed until production access is approved.
  • Step-by-step Play Console setup guidance for first-time publishers. We never need your login.
  • Short, direct progress updates the whole way.

Who is behind it

KubeAI is an independent maker of AI-assisted products; you may know the @KubeAI YouTube channel or the ScrollRSI capture app. 12 Play Testers applies the same care to the paperwork Google asks for before an app can go live.
