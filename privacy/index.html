Privacy

Last updated 30 October 2026.

What this site collects

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing that tracks you; even the font is served from this site itself. The only data it collects is what you type into an inquiry form: app name, category, Play Console status, how you describe yourself, your email address, your preferred contact channel, a number or username if you choose to give one, and your message.

What happens to an inquiry

Inquiries are delivered through Formspree, a form processor acting on our behalf, and we read them at quotes@kubeai.info. An inquiry is used for one thing: to reply with a quote and, if you go ahead, to run your testing job. We do not add inquiries to a marketing list and we do not send newsletters.

How Formspree handles submission data is described in Formspree's privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting

We keep inquiries only as long as we need them to handle them. To have your inquiry deleted, email privacy@kubeai.info and we will delete it.

Scope

This policy covers play.kubeai.info only. Other KubeAI sites, such as kubeai.info and scrollrsi.kubeai.info, publish their own policies.
